2. Core Intake, Grading & Value Chain Calibration
The platform treats quality and grading data as immutable parameters that govern trade validity. Each value chain
possesses discrete biological and physical constraints that dictate system behavior, storage degradation rules, and
contract parameters.

2.1 Maize (Zea mays) & Rice (Oryza sativa) Value Chains
Grading Standards: Conforms strictly to EAS 2:2013 (Maize) and EAS 128:2013 (Rice). The system enforces
mandatory entry variables at intake:
Moisture Content limit: Max 13.5% for Maize; 14.0% for Rice (system flags and rejects ingestion if moisture
data exceeds thresholds).
Foreign Matter: Max 1.0%; Broken Grains: Max 2.0%; Insect Damaged Grains: Max 1.0%.
Batching & Lot Mechanics: Grains are treated as Fungible Commodities. Intake lots matching identical
grade and moisture variables are dynamically aggregated into unified warehouse silos. The e-WR issued
reflects fractional ownership of an identical pooled volume (V_{pool}).
2.2 Coffee (Coffea arabica / canephora) Value Chain
Grading Standards: Structured by bean size (Screen sizes: AA, AB, PB, C) and liquoring/cupping quality
scores (Scale 1–10 based on acidity, body, and flavor profile).
Batching & Lot Mechanics: Treated as Semi-Fungible Bundles. Coffee cannot be pooled across different
cooperatives or single estates due to severe origin-price variances. The system enforces micro-lot isolation,
pinning the e-WR to a specific, traceably sealed pallet batch within the certified facility.
2.3 Tea (Camellia sinensis) Value Chain
Grading Standards: Categorized by processing type (CTC vs. Orthodox) and primary leaf grades: Broken
Orange Pekoe (BOP), Broken Orange Pekoe Fannings (BOPF), Dust 1 (D1), and Pekoe Fannings (PF).
Batching & Lot Mechanics: Treated as Non-Fungible Documented Batches. Tea is batched by unique
"Invoices" representing a specific tea factory factory run and factory mark. The digital system maintains
absolute tracing parameters connecting the invoice serial number to the central registry ID.

2.4 Avocado (Persea americana) Value Chain – Extreme Perishable Protocol
Grading Standards: Governed by EAS 19:2017. Dictated by variety (Hass vs. Fuerte), sizing codes (10 to 30
based on gram weight), and cold-chain compliance metrics.
Batching & Lot Mechanics: Strict Time-Decaying Batches. Avocados cannot utilize standard long-term
warehouse storage receipts. The platform isolates avocado intake into Controlled Atmosphere (CA) coldstorage lots with an active countdown timer attribute (t_{expiry} = 30 ext{ days}). The platform updates a
degradation coefficient daily, affecting base valuation models.
