4. The Market Dynamics Engine
Once an e-WR is mapped to the user dashboard, the owner activates one of three programmatic transaction tracks.
The platform logic manages state transitions, locking mechanics, and settlement execution paths.
4.1 Engine A: Spot Marketplace (Fixed-Price Clearing)
The spot engine acts as an instantaneous business-to-business electronic billboard matching immediate demand
against fixed pricing parameters.
Locking Protocol: The moment a buyer clicks "Execute Order," the target e-WR state transitions to
LOCKED_PENDING_SETTLEMENT within our system database. A state change call is pushed to the WRSC
Registry to prevent concurrent actions.
Settlement Window: The buyer has a hard stop of T_{settle} = 60 ext{ minutes} to complete payment processing
via mobile money or bank transfer. If checkout is incomplete at expiration, the state rolls back to AVAILABLE.
4.2 Engine B: Live Digital Auction (Dynamic Price Discovery)
Auctions implement a time-bound ascending price discovery mechanism suited for specialized micro-lots or macropooling clearings.
Bidding Parameters: The seller defines the base reserve price (P_{reserve}) and the bidding interval time.
Bidders submit electronic binding quotes. Each bid must exceed the previous high quote by a minimum delta
(\Delta P \ge 1.5\%).
Anti-Sniper Mechanism: If a valid bid is logged within the final 3 ext{ minutes} of the scheduled auction
countdown, the system triggers a dynamic extension rule, adding exactly 3 ext{ minutes} to the expiration clock
(T_{end} = T_{current} + 3) until bidding ceases.

4.3 Engine C: Forward Contract Engine (Structured Term Deliveries)
The Forward Contract Engine facilitates future security of supply without entering the domain of financial option
derivatives. These are fixed-date, physical delivery obligations.
Contract Specification: Agreements must specify Maturity Date (D_{maturity}), Delivery Price (P_{delivery}),
and Performance Bond Requirements (B_{premium}).
Default Risk Mitigation via Performance Bonds: To ensure trade adherence without a formal central
clearinghouse license, the platform enforces mutual escrow bonds. Upon contract bilateral signing, both the
Buyer and Seller must lock a liquidity percentage into the bank escrow:
B_{premium} = P_{delivery} imes 15\%
If the Buyer fails to settle the remaining balance at maturity, the escrow system auto-forfeits the buyer's 15%
performance bond directly to the farmer's wallet as liquidated damages. Conversely, if the seller tampers with or
unauthorized withdraws stock through collusion, their bond pays out to the buyer.
