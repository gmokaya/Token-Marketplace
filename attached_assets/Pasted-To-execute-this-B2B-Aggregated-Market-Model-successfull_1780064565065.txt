To execute this **B2B Aggregated Market Model** successfully, your tech stack must prioritize data integrity, rock-solid security, and low-latency API handling rather than flashy consumer UI.

Since you are skipping tokenization, you don’t need complex web3 blockchain nodes. Instead, you need a high-performance **event-driven architecture** that handles row-level database locking (to prevent double-selling) and interfaces cleanly with banking systems and the government’s **WRSC Central Registry**.

Here is the production-ready tech stack designed to build this system safely, efficiently, and at a minimal starting cost.

---

## 1. Backend & Business Logic Layer (The Engine)

Your backend must handle complex workflow routing (Spot, Auctions, Forwards) and execute real-time arithmetic calculations for split payments and loan interest.

* **Primary Framework:** **Node.js (NestJS)** or **Go (Golang)**.
* *Why NestJS:* It provides a highly structured TypeScript environment, native support for microservices, and handles asynchronous events (like auction countdowns) incredibly well.
* *Why Go:* If you expect massive concurrency during live, fast-paced auctions, Go offers unmatched speed and light memory usage.


* **Workflow Engine:** **Temporal.io** or **BullMQ**.
* *Why:* Forward contracts mature weeks or months in the future. Temporal allows you to manage long-running, durable state machines that won't fail or lose track of a contract even if your server restarts.



---

## 2. Database Tier (The Single Source of Truth)

Because warehouse receipts and escrow transactions require 100% data durability, your database configuration is the most critical technical choice.

* **Core Database:** **PostgreSQL**.
* *Why:* You need strict ACID compliance. PostgreSQL allows you to implement `SERIALIZABLE` transaction isolation levels and row-level locks (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`). This guarantees that if two millers click "Buy Now" on the exact same maize receipt at the exact same millisecond, only one transaction succeeds, and the other is safely rejected.


* **Cache & Live Queue Layer:** **Redis Enterprise**.
* *Why:* Used to power your **Live Auction Engine**. When buyers are rapidly outbidding each other in the final minutes of a tea or coffee auction, handling those bids in PostgreSQL would crush your database. Redis stores the bidding queue in-memory for instant feedback, then flushes the final winning bid to PostgreSQL when the timer hits zero.



---

## 3. Integration & API Layer (The Bridges)

Your platform’s true value lies in acting as the data pipe connecting the WRSC Registry, the Bank, and the Cooperatives.

* **API Management:** **GraphQL** or **RESTful APIs with OpenAPI/Swagger**.
* *Why:* GraphQL is highly efficient for your frontend dashboards (e.g., pulling a nested payload showing a factory's active e-WRs, along with their grading data and active bank liens in a single call).


* **Webhook Listener:** Dedicated, high-availability endpoints built on **AWS Lambda** or **Google Cloud Functions**.
* *Why:* When the WRSC Central Registry registers a title transfer or shifts a receipt to "encumbered," it will ping your platform via a webhook. These serverless entry points ensure you never miss a state update from the government registry.



---

## 4. Frontend & Mobile Layer (The Dashboards)

You are building dashboards for professional business users (Factory managers, Bank credit officers, Commodity buyers). They need high-density data layouts, not complex animations.

* **Web Dashboards:** **React.js** with **Next.js** or **Vue.js**.
* *Why:* Next.js provides excellent performance and static state management for handling heavy data tables, complex filtering (sorting maize by moisture, grading, location), and live charting for market price dynamics.


* **Mobile App (For Cooperative/Factory Managers):** **Flutter** or **React Native**.
* *Why:* Cooperative managers are often out in the field or at the factory floor. A cross-platform mobile framework allows you to deploy a single codebase to Android and iOS. Flutter is particularly excellent at handling real-time data sync and offline caching if internet drops at a remote sorting hub.


* **UI Component Library:** **Tailwind CSS** paired with **Chakra UI** or **Shadcn UI**.
* *Why:* Speeds up development by providing clean, pre-built B2B data tables, modal forms, and metrics dashboards out of the box.



---

## 5. Security & Infrastructure Layer (The Vault)

Because you are orchestrating heavy financial instructions, security breaches mean massive legal liabilities.

* **Cloud Hosting:** **AWS (Amazon Web Services)** or **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**.
* *Why:* They offer pre-certified compliance environments. You must deploy your architecture inside an isolated **Virtual Private Cloud (VPC)**.


* **Financial Grade Security:** **OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect** via **Auth0** or **Keycloak**.
* *Why:* Enforces mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for high-clearance actions like a bank officer releasing escrow capital or a factory manager moving an asset to auction.


* **Payload Cryptography:** **AES-256 GCM encryption** for data-at-rest; **TLS 1.3** for all data-in-transit. When communicating with the bank’s core API, you will likely need to implement **mTLS (Mutual TLS)** where both your server and the bank server cryptographically verify each other before passing payloads.

| **Durable Workflows** | Temporal.io | Managing long-term 90-day forward contracts. |
