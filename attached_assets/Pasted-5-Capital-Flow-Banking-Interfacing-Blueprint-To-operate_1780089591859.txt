5. Capital Flow & Banking Interfacing Blueprint
To operate within absolute compliance boundaries, the platform segregates its processing into separate transaction
and payout sequences using automated API-driven instructions executed by a partner commercial bank.
5.1 Pre-Sale Liquidity Financing Mechanics
If a farmer requires liquidity immediately following WRS ingestion but prior to market matching, they execute an
integrated Inventory Receipt Discounting sequence:
1. Evaluation Engine
The platform calculates the risk-adjusted Maximum Advance Liquidity (L_{max}) based on the asset's current
market valuation (V_{market}) and a 40% haircut safety buffer:
L_{max} = V_{market} imes (1 - 0.40) = V_{market} imes 0.60
▼
2. Bank Capital Ingress
The integrated partner bank approves the payload via API, creating a loan record inside the bank core ledger
and transferring L_{max} directly to the farmer's mobile money/bank wallet.
▼
3. Registry Encumbrance Locking
The platform transmits an automated call to the eWRS-CR registry endpoint, triggering a status update to
STATUS_ENCUMBERED and designating the partner bank as the prime lienholder. The receipt cannot be
transferred or traded without automated loan liquidation clauses.
5.2 Granular Multi-Party Settlement Split Sequence
When an asset transitions to a closed trade state (such as an auction clearing or forward contract maturity), the
escrow ledger executes an isolated split sequence. Let V_{total} represent the final gross purchase capital
deposited by the buyer into the transaction escrow account, L_{advance} represent the outstanding principal loan
amount, r represent the bank's annualized interest rate, and t represent the exact holding period in days.
The complete distribution of funds is governed by the following programmatic routing equations:
Bank Liquidation Return (R_{bank}):
R_{bank} = L_{advance} imes \left(1 + rac{r imes t}{365} ight)

Platform Operational Revenue Fee (F_{platform}):
F_{platform} = V_{total} imes 2\% = V_{total} imes 0.02
Net Producer Residual Payout (P_{producer}):
P_{producer} = V_{total} - R_{bank} - F_{platform}
