To successfully operate an **Aggregated B2B Marketplace** integrated with a state-backed central registry and commercial bank escrow accounts, your onboarding profile fields must be strictly designed around corporate identity, regulatory compliance, and transactional verification.

Because you are bypassing individual smallholders, you are collecting data on **legal entities and commercial businesses**. This structure filters out fraud and automates transaction routing.

---

## 1. TIER 1: The Producers (Factories & Cooperatives)

*System Objective:* Verify that the entity is a legally registered cooperative or factory mark, and link their account to the exact identity parameters used by the **WRSC Central Registry**.

### General Identity & KYB

* **Registered Entity Name:** (e.g., *Nyeri Coffee Farmers Co-operative Society Ltd*).
* **Registration Number / Certificate of Incorporation:** Issued by the Business Registration Service (BRS) or the Commissioner for Co-operatives.
* **Corporate KRA PIN:** For tax validation and automatic generation of withholding tax logs or platform invoice billing.
* **Registered Office Address & Factory/Silo Locations:** Physical GPS coordinates of the primary cooperative hubs.

### Contact & Operational Authorizations

* **Authorized System Administrator Profile:** First Name, Last Name, National ID/Passport Number, Official Email, and Phone Number. (This is the specific individual authorized to list inventory and accept auction bids).
* **Factory Marks / Codes:** For Coffee and Tea value chains, collect their official unique identifier stamps used globally by brokers and auctions.

### Banking & Mobile Money Ingress (Payout Endpoints)

* **Cooperative Settlement Corporate Bank Account:** Bank Name, Branch, Swift Code, and Account Number.
* **Corporate Paybill / Till Number or Designated Mobile Money Wallet:** For instant split-payout routing under the maximum transaction thresholds.

## 3. TIER 3: The Enablers (Warehouse Operators)

*System Objective:* Ensure the storage hub is legally allowed to store, grade, and secure collateral under the Warehouse Receipt System Act.

### Legal & Regulatory Anchors

* **Operator Company Name:** (e.g., *AgriBora Certified Silos* or *NCPB Eldoret Depot*).
* **Official WRSC License Number:** The structural operator license number issued by the Warehouse Receipt System Council.
* **WRSC Central Registry System API Key / Client Identifier:** The structural endpoint token allowing your platform to read their specific e-WR issuance events.

### Structural Details (Database Parameters)

* **Warehouse Facility Type & Capacity:** (e.g., *Dry Grain Silo, 10,000 Metric Ton Capacity* or *Controlled Atmosphere Cold Storage*).
* **List of Approved Handled Value Chains:** Select checkboxes matching your marketplace options (Maize, Rice, Coffee, Tea).
* **Structural and Collateral Insurance Policy Numbers:** Insurer Name, Underwritten Value Limit, and Expiry Date (to ensure the crops are covered against fire, theft, or spoilage while transactions clear).

### Operations Contact

* **Head Warehouse In-Charge / Collateral Manager:** Direct phone number and secure platform system email (for automated notification dispatch when an e-WR owner changes).

## Technical Onboarding Architecture Recommendation

When designing your database schema in **PostgreSQL**, use a base `users` table linked via a one-to-one relationship to distinct profile tables based on role designation: `producer_profiles`, `buyer_profiles`, `warehouse_profiles`, and `financier_profiles`.

Ensure that during the registration phase, your backend implements a **Status Workflow** tracking field (`PENDING_KYB_APPROVAL` $\rightarrow$ `WRSC_VERIFIED` $\rightarrow$ `ACTIVE`). A user profile must only shift to `ACTIVE` once their corporate registration numbers match clean results from integrated business registries and the WRSC database via your validation endpoints.
