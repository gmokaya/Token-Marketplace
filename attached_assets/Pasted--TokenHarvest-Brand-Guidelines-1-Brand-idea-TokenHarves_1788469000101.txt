# TokenHarvest Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand idea

**TokenHarvest helps useful people find meaningful ways to contribute to African agricultural commodities and trade.**

The brand should feel:

- Useful, not performative
- Human, not corporate
- Optimistic, not overly polished
- Grounded, not generic
- Clear, calm, and operational
- Community-oriented rather than job-application-oriented

Primary brand thought:

> **Bring your useful instinct.**

Use **community** rather than “network.” Use **contributor** rather than “applicant” wherever possible.

---

## 2. Approved color palette

### Deep Green — Primary brand color

`#073B35`

Use for:

- Main headings
- Primary buttons
- Navigation and dark surfaces
- Card backgrounds
- Logo on light backgrounds
- Important text

This is the foundational TokenHarvest color.

### Bright Lemon Yellow — Signal color

`#DFFC4A`

Use for:

- Highlights
- Selected states
- Progress indicators
- Small decorative details
- Key calls to action
- Card accents

Use deep green text on yellow. Do not use white text on yellow.

### Soft White — Main surface color

`#F6F4EF`

Use for:

- Page backgrounds
- Light text on deep green
- Large open surfaces
- Soft card details
- Background contrast

This should make the experience feel warm rather than stark.

### Cold Grey — Supporting color

`#808A87`

Use for:

- Borders
- Input outlines
- Muted surfaces
- Decorative lines
- Secondary UI
- Card pattern elements
- Quiet metadata

Avoid using cold grey for small body text on soft white because the contrast is too weak. Use deep green for readable text.

---

## 3. Recommended color balance

A typical TokenHarvest screen should use approximately:

- **60%** soft white
- **25%** deep green
- **10%** cold grey
- **5%** bright lemon yellow

Yellow should feel like a signal, not a background replacement.

### Avoid

- Orange or coral as primary accents
- Generic SaaS blue
- Large areas of bright yellow
- Multiple competing accent colors
- Low-contrast grey body text
- White text directly on yellow

---

## 4. Logo and wordmark

The wordmark is:

> **TokenHarvest**

Capitalization:

- Capital **T**
- Capital **H**
- All other letters lowercase

Typography:

- **Belleza**
- Regular weight
- Natural letter spacing
- No forced uppercase
- No extra tracking

The wordmark should normally appear in deep green on soft white or soft white on deep green.

The wordmark is also the home link in the onboarding experience.

### Clear space

Keep at least the height of the capital **T** around the wordmark on all sides.

### Do not

- Stretch or compress the wordmark
- Add shadows, outlines, or gradients
- Change the capitalization
- Place it over a busy image
- Use yellow as the default wordmark color

---

## 5. Typography

### Brand wordmark

**Belleza**

Used only for the TokenHarvest wordmark.

### Headlines

**Simplo**

Use Simplo for:

- Hero headlines
- Section headings
- Results and profile statements
- Large expressive text
- Short emphasized phrases

Headlines should have:

- Tight line height
- Slightly tight letter spacing
- Strong contrast
- Occasional italic emphasis for a human, editorial feel

### Body and interface text

**Outfit**

Use Outfit for:

- Paragraphs
- Buttons
- Form labels
- Helper text
- Navigation
- Metadata
- Status text

Body copy should remain clear, conversational, and easy to scan.

---

## 6. Layout and shape language

TokenHarvest uses a soft, editorial interface style.

Preferred characteristics:

- Generous whitespace
- Rounded pill-shaped buttons
- Softly rounded cards
- Light borders
- Large, confident headlines
- Calm single-viewport layouts
- Subtle motion and floating elements
- Clear visual hierarchy

Avoid overly dense dashboards, sharp rectangular controls, or excessive decorative elements.

---

## 7. UNO-style welcome card

The welcome card is an abstract agricultural-trade motif, not a literal game-brand reproduction.

Approved card colors:

- Deep green base: `#073B35`
- Bright lemon yellow accents: `#DFFC4A`
- Cold grey pattern elements: `#808A87`
- Soft white highlights: `#F6F4EF`

The card may use:

- Thin curved linework
- Abstract geometric fields
- Small yellow signal marks
- Subtle grain or paper texture
- Slight rotation and floating motion

Keep the card expressive but secondary to the main message.

---

## 8. Voice and tone

### Voice

- Direct
- Warm
- Useful
- Thoughtful
- Confident
- Grounded in real work

### Prefer

- “Where do you feel useful?”
- “Bring your useful instinct.”
- “Enter the community.”
- “What could you help move forward?”
- “People building something worth joining.”

### Avoid

- “Submit your application”
- “Optimize your professional profile”
- “Join our talent network”
- “Disrupt the market”
- Overly corporate or recruitment-heavy language
- Empty startup clichés

---

## 9. Interaction principles

Every interaction should feel intentional and low-friction.

Use:

- Clear progress indicators
- One main decision per screen
- Pill-shaped controls
- Helpful, concise feedback
- Strong selected states
- Visible back navigation
- Friendly empty and error states

Yellow can indicate selection or momentum, but status meaning should never rely on color alone. Pair important states with text, icons, or labels.

---

## 10. Accessibility rules

- Use deep green text on soft white for primary readability.
- Use deep green text on lemon yellow.
- Do not use cold grey as small text on soft white.
- Do not use white text on lemon yellow.
- Ensure selected states have both color and structural distinction.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus states.
- Do not communicate success, warning, or error through color alone.
- Keep body text at a comfortable reading size and line height.

---

## 11. Quick do and don’t list

### Do

- Use deep green as the visual anchor.
- Use lemon yellow sparingly as a signal.
- Use cold grey for quiet supporting details.
- Keep the tone human and contribution-focused.
- Combine strong Simplo headlines with readable Outfit body copy.
- Use agricultural, field, trade, and community references thoughtfully.

### Don’t

- Bring back orange or coral as a primary accent.
- Overuse yellow.
- Use grey for important text.
- Make the experience feel like a conventional job application.
- Add decorative details that compete with the invitation.
- Use generic blue SaaS styling.
