Here's the complete wizard, start to finish, with the needs question rebuilt as multi-select "select all that apply" chips using your actual categories, and the social enterprise tone carried through the copy.

```
Build a Next.js 14 (App Router) web app for TokenHarvest, a social 
enterprise and agri-fintech marketplace connecting Producers, Traders, 
and Buyers of specialty agricultural commodities in East Africa. Use 
TypeScript, Clerk for authentication, Tailwind CSS for styling, and 
Postgres (via Prisma) for the database.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES (apply throughout)
- Mobile-first: Producers especially may sign up from a phone with 
  variable connectivity. Every screen must work at 360px width first.
- Progressive disclosure: ask only what's needed to get someone in; 
  defer the rest.
- Always show progress: a step indicator ("Step 1 of 2") during 
  onboarding.
- Every action has a visible state: loading, success, error — no 
  silent failures.
- Inline validation as users type/blur, not just on submit.
- Accessible by default: label/input pairing, visible focus states, 
  color contrast, keyboard navigable throughout.
- Autosave/resume: if someone drops off mid-profile-form, their 
  progress is saved.
- Plain, warm language. Avoid jargon like "KYC" in user-facing 
  copy — say "Verify your account." As a social enterprise, tone 
  should read as "we're building this with you," not corporate or 
  transactional.

## USER ROLES
Three account categories, chosen at signup: Producer, Trader, Buyer. 
(Warehouse Operators use a separate internal portal — out of scope.)

## ONBOARDING WIZARD

### Step 0 — Role Selection
Three large, tappable cards, each with a one-line description of 
what that role can do. Above the cards, a short mission line: 
"TokenHarvest connects farmers, traders, and buyers across East 
Africa — built to make trade fairer for everyone in the chain." 
No auth yet.

### Step 1 — Authentication (Clerk)
Same <SignUp /> widget for all three roles. Support email AND 
phone/OTP — default to phone-first for Producers, email-first for 
Trader/Buyer. Show a reassurance line: "Takes about 2 minutes — you 
can finish the rest later."

Immediately after account creation, use a Clerk webhook 
(`user.created`) — server-side only, never client-side — to set:
  {
    role: "producer" | "trader" | "buyer",
    kycStatus: "not_started"
  }

### Step 2 — Category-Specific Profile
Short forms (4-5 fields max), large touch-friendly inputs, correct 
mobile keyboard types, inline validation with specific error messages.

Producer:
  - Full name
  - Region/county (searchable dropdown)
  - Commodities grown (multi-select chips)
  - Payout method: mobile money number

Trader:
  - Business name
  - Business registration number
  - Commodities traded (multi-select chips)
  - Bank details for escrow payouts

Buyer:
  - Company name
  - Business registration number
  - Sourcing interests (commodity + expected volume)
  - Destination country

### Step 2.5 — "Tell Us What Matters to You" (multi-select, optional 
but shown by default)

Frame this warmly and tie it to mission, not data collection:
  Heading: "One quick thing 😊"
  Subtext: "Select as many as apply — this helps us build 
  TokenHarvest around what actually matters to farmers, traders, 
  and buyers like you."

Render as tappable, multi-select pill/chip buttons (not checkboxes 
in a list — chips feel lighter and more casual). Store selections 
as a string array field `interests` on the user's profile record.

PRODUCER — "What would help you most right now? (select all that apply)"
  - Access to markets → access_to_markets
  - Better prices → better_prices
  - Reducing post-harvest losses → reduce_post_harvest_losses
  - Better quality storage → quality_storage
  - Access to financing → access_to_finance
  - Faster payments → faster_payments

  Below the chips, add ONE optional open text field, clearly marked 
  optional and low-pressure:
  "Want to share a bit of your story? (optional)" — a short textarea, 
  placeholder: "e.g. how long you've been farming, what you grow, 
  what you're hoping for." Store as `producerStory` (nullable text). 
  Note in a comment: this is for the social enterprise's impact 
  storytelling/marketing use, not required for platform function.

TRADER — "What's on your mind these days? (select all that apply)"
  - Finding more buyers → find_buyers
  - Freeing up capital tied in stock → free_up_capital
  - Getting paid faster → faster_payment
  - Access to trade financing → access_to_financing
  - Simplifying logistics → simplify_logistics

BUYER — "What matters most to you? (select all that apply)"
Render as two visually distinct groups under one heading, since these 
are genuinely different types of motivation and grouping them helps 
buyers scan faster:

  Group 1 — "Values & Impact"
    - Fair trade practices → fair_trade
    - Traceable sourcing → traceable_sourcing
    - Social & environmental impact → impact
    - Direct trade with producers → direct_trade
    - Producer welfare → producer_welfare

  Group 2 — "Commercial Priorities"
    - Trade financing → trade_financing
    - Secure payments → secure_payments
    - Reliable logistics → reliable_logistics
    - Insurance coverage → insurance
    - Access to verified producers → access_to_producers
    - Verified quality → verified_quality
    - Consistent supply → consistent_supply
    - GI-certified / origin-specific products → gi_certification
    - Overall quality assurance → quality_assurance

On completion of Step 2 (including 2.5), set kycStatus to 
"profile_complete," show a warm success state ("You're in! 🎉 
Welcome to TokenHarvest."), then redirect to the dashboard.

## STEPS 3 & 4 ARE DEFERRED — NOT PART OF SIGNUP

Do not block signup on document upload or review. After Step 2, the 
user lands in their dashboard immediately with limited access.

Show a friendly, dismissible verification card on the dashboard 
(never a blocking modal): "Verify your account to unlock the rest of 
TokenHarvest — like listing your harvest and accessing financing. 
Takes about 5 minutes." Frame verification as protecting the whole 
community's trust, not as a compliance hurdle: e.g. "This helps us 
keep TokenHarvest fair and safe for everyone trading here."

### /verify route — Step 3 (Document Upload)
Role-specific checklist with a one-line reason per document (e.g. 
"We use this to confirm you're a licensed trader"). Show per-file 
upload progress and success confirmation. Allow partial completion 
and resuming later.
  - Producer: National ID
  - Trader: Business registration certificate, trading license
  - Buyer: Business registration certificate, import certifications 
    (optional at this stage)

Set kycStatus to "submitted" once all required docs for the role are 
uploaded. Show a clear "What happens next" message with an expected 
review timeframe.

### Admin Review — Step 4
Minimal /admin/kyc route, protected by a real Clerk admin role (not 
a hardcoded flag), listing users with kycStatus "submitted," their 
documents, and Approve/Reject actions.
  - Approve → kycStatus = "approved" → notify user (email/SMS): 
    "You're verified! Full access to TokenHarvest is now unlocked."
  - Reject → kycStatus = "rejected" with a required reason field, 
    shown to the user with clear, kind next steps to resubmit.

## ACCESS CONTROL LOGIC

Middleware/helper checks publicMetadata.kycStatus on protected 
ACTIONS, not entire pages — let everyone browse:

- "not_started" / "profile_complete" → full read access (browse 
  listings, prices, how-it-works). Write actions (list a commodity, 
  place a bid, request financing) show a friendly inline prompt: 
  "Verify your account to do this" linking to /verify — never a 
  dead-end error.
- "submitted" → same restrictions, banner reads "Verification in 
  review — usually takes [X]."
- "approved" → full access unlocked for that role's actions.
- "rejected" → clear specific reason shown, prominent "Resubmit" CTA.

## PERSONALIZATION USING `interests`

On dashboard load, check the user's `interests` array and surface 
the most relevant feature card first:
  - Producer with "access_to_finance" or "faster_payments" selected 
    → Harvest Fund card leads.
  - Producer with "reduce_post_harvest_losses" or "quality_storage" 
    → lead with warehouse/storage-related content once built.
  - Trader with "free_up_capital" or "access_to_financing" → Fast 
    Pay card leads.
  - Buyer with "trade_financing" → Order Finance card leads.
  - Buyer with values-group selections (fair_trade, traceable_sourcing, 
    impact, direct_trade, producer_welfare) → surface impact/origin 
    storytelling content (e.g. producer stories, GI/traceability 
    info) prominently, not just commercial listings.

Store `interests` as structured data so it can be aggregated later 
for product decisions and impact reporting (e.g. "X% of buyers care 
most about producer welfare" is a genuinely useful stat for a social 
enterprise's own reporting, not just personalization).

## DASHBOARDS (per role)
Shared layout shell, role-specific content:
  - Producer: My Stock, My eWRs, Harvest Fund status, verification 
    banner, personalized feature card based on interests
  - Trader: My Warehouse Deposits, Active Listings, Fast Pay status, 
    verification banner, personalized feature card
  - Buyer: Marketplace Browse, My Orders, Order Finance status, 
    verification banner, personalized feature card

Empty states matter: new users see a helpful first action ("List 
your first harvest" with a button), never a blank screen.

## SECURITY NOTES
- All kycStatus, role, and interests writes happen server-side 
  (webhook or server action) — never trust client-side metadata 
  updates.
- Validate file uploads server-side (type, size limits).
- Rate-limit OTP requests and form submissions.

Prioritize a fast, low-friction, warm-feeling onboarding flow and 
airtight access-gating logic over visual polish. Functional and 
welcoming beats flashy for this prototype.
```
