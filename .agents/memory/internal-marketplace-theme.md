---
name: Internal marketplace theme
description: The visual boundary between public market pages and signed-in marketplace workspaces
---

Signed-in Grain, Coffee, and Tea workspaces use the attached Material Dashboard direction: light neutral canvas, white rounded surfaces, compact information-dense layouts, and subtle shadows. Their sidebars are deliberately market-colored (slate for Grain, espresso for Coffee, forest for Tea) with high-contrast navigation and a restrained active state. Keep each commodity's accent color for charts, statuses, and small identity cues. Public market landing pages remain editorial and image-led.

**Why:** The internal product benefits from scanability and consistency, while the public pages need a stronger commodity-specific first impression.

**How to apply:** Change shared internal shell tokens, colored sidebar, header, and shadcn surface styling together across all three marketplace artifacts. Do not flatten the public Grain/Coffee/Tea hero imagery into the dashboard theme unless explicitly requested.
